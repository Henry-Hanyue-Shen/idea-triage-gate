# Third-party notices and design lineage

Idea Triage Gate is an original skill implementation. It does not include third-party source code, model weights, datasets, private prompts, or copied skill instructions.

Its design was informed at a high level by public research and open-source themes previously reviewed by the maintainer, including:

- idea refinement and evidence-gate workflows;
- academic language and methodology auditing;
- sparse or dependency-derived architectures;
- distributional prediction and causal reconstruction;
- versioned memory and multi-agent organization;
- quantized, tiny, and edge-deployed models;
- large-document decomposition, public APIs, and tool orchestration; and
- software patterns for bounded, machine-readable handoffs.

The repository's `references/pattern-library.md` intentionally expresses these as generic design prompts. It does not reproduce third-party code or claim that social-media descriptions, benchmark numbers, product announcements, or remembered project details are verified facts. Users must consult current primary sources before relying on any resulting novelty, technical, legal, or commercial conclusion.
